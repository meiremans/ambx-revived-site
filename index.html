Your amBX lights, back on.

amBX Revived is an independent Windows 11 app for the Philips amBX kit. It matches your lights to your screen, your music and your games.

Set up your kit See what it does

Five lights, three ways to use them

The app drives all five zones of the kit: the left and right speakers, and the left, center and right wall lights.

Screen ambient

The lights follow the edges of the display you choose. Black bars in movies are ignored, and you set the brightness, saturation and transition speed.

Music

Bass, mids and highs drive the lights while the colors move through the spectrum. It works with any playback device, including Bluetooth headphones, or a microphone.

Game Flash

The lights flash on sharp sounds such as gunfire and impacts. You can listen to one game only, keep screen colors between flashes, and save a profile per game.

Your setup

Set fixed colors, calibrate each lamp, and switch modes from the tray or with global shortcuts. Profiles can start by themselves when a game launches.

What you need

  • A Philips amBX kit with its USB effects controller
  • Windows 11 on an x64 PC
  • A one-time setup in Device Manager, using a driver already in Windows

Lights only for now. The kit's fans and wrist rumble bar aren't supported yet.

No kit to hand? Demo mode shows every effect on screen.

Your screen stays on your PC

There's no account, no analytics and no upload. Your screen and audio are turned into light colors, then discarded. Read the privacy policy.

Photosensitivity warning: Game Flash and other effects can produce flashing lights.
