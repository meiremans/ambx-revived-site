Privacy policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

amBX Revived controls the lights of a Philips amBX kit. It runs on your PC and has no account. It does not send information to the developer or to anyone else.

What the app accesses

DataWhenHow it's used
Your screenScreen ambient, and Game Flash with ambient onFrames are reduced to colors for the lights, then discarded. Nothing is saved.
Audio playing on your PCMusic, Game Flash and Test audioAudio is measured for levels and frequencies, then discarded. Nothing is recorded.
Your microphoneOnly if you choose a microphone as the audio sourceHandled the same way as playback audio. Nothing is recorded.
Names of running programsWhen you choose a game for game-only audio or a profile, and while a profile is set to start with a gameUsed to find the game on your PC.

What the app stores

The app saves your settings on your PC, including presets, game profiles, lamp calibration and shortcuts. Settings can include audio device IDs and game file names. Settings stay on your PC unless you export a backup, which goes to a file you choose.

What the app shares

The app has no analytics, advertising or crash reporting, and it does not send your data over the internet. Its interface communicates only with the app itself on your PC.

Microsoft handles Store downloads and updates under its own privacy statement.

Your controls

  • Stop any effect to stop capture.
  • Choose a playback source rather than a microphone.
  • Turn off microphone access for the app in Windows Settings under Privacy & security.
  • Uninstall the app to remove it. Windows removes its stored settings with it.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics or scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitor information such as IP addresses under its own privacy statement.
